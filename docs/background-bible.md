## Lighting by act

1. Warm daylight + amber cooking light
2. Slightly darker kitchen light (greed creeping in)
3. Neutral afternoon, less saturation
4. Grand golden ceremony light vs spoiled food
5. Neutral dramatic investigation light
6. Soft golden morning/afternoon (fresh start)
